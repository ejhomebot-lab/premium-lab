The % above each bar is the average annual net return: the cumulative net return % divided by the number of years.

Year by year

Net return % is the net return as a percentage of the client's own cash. Avg annual return is the net return % divided by the number of years, the same figure shown above each bar. IRR is the annual rate of return on the client's actual cash flows: own cash paid at the start, loan interest paid each year, and the value left after repaying the loan in that year.
